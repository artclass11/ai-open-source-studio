# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Expected behavior includes constructive feedback, respect for different experiences, and responsible handling of security and privacy concerns. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, malicious automation, and publishing private information.

Report concerns privately through the security contact mechanism or repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
